ArchivePlayer logo concepts

Five concepts in the Nordic design language (blue #2769AD, white, ink #15191E). Each one is shown as an Android adaptive launcher icon in circle and squircle masks, on a light and a dark home screen, as the Android 13+ monochrome “themed icon”, and as the in-app lockup for the setup screen and top bar. Everything is vector SVG, ready for ic_launcher_foreground/monochrome.
