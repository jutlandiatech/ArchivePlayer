ArchivePlayer

An Android player for the audio and video on the Internet Archive. No account, no tracking: playlists and history stay on your phone.

  • Download the app (APK) — full includes Chromecast, foss has no Google services.
  • Source code (Apache License 2.0)

This site also hosts shared playlists (/p) and the category map the app uses for Home and Browse (catalog/category-map.json).
